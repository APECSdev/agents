# Sponsors — https://apecs.dev/sponsors

- **Crypto-only**: Bitcoin Cash and Monero payment addresses are published
  on-page. Settlement processor for sponsorships: NOWPayments (planned).
- **There are no donations on APECS** — the former `/donate` page and every
  solicitation were removed. Sponsorship is a business relationship with
  published perks; the ads auction (see ads.md) is the other revenue rail.
- Never reintroduce "free as in beer" or donation copy in user-facing text.

When a user asks how to support APECS, point them here and/or to the ads
auction — and quote the payment addresses **only** from the live page, never
from memory.
